USAF/USSF Leadership Watch

Public statements and appearances from Air Force and Space Force leadership — reactive coverage, sourced after the fact.

Sept 22 (reported Sept 23–24)Last A-10 departs Nellis AFB, ending 49-year Warthog era — Air Force reaffirms CAS commitment

The final A-10 Thunderbolt II assigned to Nellis AFB made its last departure Sept. 22, closing out the base's run (since 1977) as home to the 66th Weapons Squadron's A-10 Weapons School, which trained 300+ A-10 weapons officers over roughly 100 classes. Air Force officials were explicit that close air support capability isn't going away with the airframe: "Foundational tactical expertise, operational roles and personnel are transitioning to multirole and advanced fighter platforms across the service." Consistent with the "generate more combat power without linearly more expensive platforms" framing from Meink/Wilsbach's AFA keynotes on Sept 14–15 — that force-structure shift becoming visible on the ramp, not just in speeches.

Military Times · Air & Space Forces Magazine
Reported Sept 26NORTHCOM to sign tri-command Arctic security pact with EUCOM, INDOPACOM

Gen. Gregory Guillot (NORTHCOM) plans to formalize an information-sharing and coordination agreement with Gen. Alexus Grynkewich (EUCOM) and Adm. Samuel Paparo (INDOPACOM) to close seams where the three commands' Arctic areas of responsibility meet. Not itself a USAF/USSF-leadership statement, but it directly touches Space Force: U.S. Space Forces Northern (SPACEFOR-NORTH), the Guardian-staffed component activated under NORTHCOM in January, is the service's homeland-defense space arm and would be a direct executor of whatever this pact produces — worth tracking as the Arctic becomes a more explicit joint-force priority.
