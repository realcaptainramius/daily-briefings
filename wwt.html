USAF/USSF Leadership Watch

Public statements and appearances from Air Force and Space Force leadership — reactive coverage, sourced after the fact.

Nov 20Wilsbach presents Presidential Unit Citation to Whiteman's B-2 wings for Midnight Hammer

Gen. Wilsbach presented the Presidential Unit Citation to the 509th Bomb Wing and the Missouri Air National Guard's 131st Bomb Wing at Whiteman AFB for Operation Midnight Hammer, the June 2025 B-2 strikes on Fordow and Natanz. The Air Force says it is the first time since the Vietnam War that a bomber unit has received the award. The ceremony date is consistent across the Air Force's own release, Stars and Stripes and Air & Space Forces. Not IT-related, but it shows where the Chief of Staff's public messaging sits: combat readiness and recent operations.

Mid-NovAir Force announces Force Design 2026, which is classified, with C2, sensors, networks and autonomy at its center

The Air Force publicly announced Force Design 2026, which replaces the 2024 One Force Design. Gen. Wilsbach approved it at Fall CORONA (Sept 29–Oct 3). He calls it a "fundamental shift" in how the service approaches future joint fights. A spokesperson said the plan is classified and there is no releasable unclassified version. Public coverage stresses command-and-control and information systems that tie aircraft, sensors and weapons together, plus autonomous systems and AI. This was missed in last week's edition. One summary dates the announcement to Nov 10, and Defense News ran its story Nov 13, so the exact day is unconfirmed.

ThemeForce Design 2026 puts C2, networks and AI at the center, the layer where WWT's federal practice already sits

C2/AIThe public framing of Force Design 2026 (above) is about connecting sensors, shooters and decision-makers: command-and-control, data networks, AI and autonomy. Golden Dome's C2 layer, which its deputy director says has moved into a test campaign, follows the same pattern. That is closer to WWT's strengths (network integration, AI infrastructure and proving-ground labs, cyber operations for the 688th CW) than platform-centric priorities would be. Because the design is classified, there is no public requirements document to map against, and nothing from WWT references Force Design 2026. Inference only.

ThemeCR expires Dec 11 (under three weeks out); talks have stalled over defense money and leaders reportedly prefer another stopgap

BudgetReporting that the continuing resolution blocks new starts across 150+ programs (and leaves Golden Dome without a CR baseline) suggests near-term demand shifts toward sustainment, existing vehicles and already-funded awards — the kind of vehicles WWT recently won (USPS IDIQ, Army GEMSS 2.1, the 688th CW contract) — rather than net-new program awards. With the Nov 3 midterms over, Congress is back for a lame-duck session to decide between full-year FY27 appropriations and another CR, and the FY27 NDAA is still pending (a House–Senate Armed Services compromise is being negotiated, with no text released as of Nov 23). Multiple outlets now report that talks stalled after the administration sought an additional $350B in defense spending tied to the Iran war, that Democrats are split over using the deadline as leverage, and that leaders on both sides lean toward another stopgap, possibly into February or March. That would extend the new-start freeze into spring. DefenseScoop (Nov 19) reports Golden Dome is proceeding on previously obligated funds for now. The Space Force growth Schiess described earlier this month depends on how this resolves. Inference only; nothing from WWT addresses the CR directly.
