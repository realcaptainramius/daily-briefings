USAF/USSF Leadership Watch

Public statements and appearances from Air Force and Space Force leadership — reactive coverage, sourced after the fact.

Oct 1Space Force retires the last two Defense Support Program missile-warning satellites

After final maneuver exercises during U.S. Space Command's Apollo Maneuvers 2026 in September, Guardians of the 2nd Space Warning Squadron moved the last two DSP satellites into graveyard orbit, ending a program that began in 1970. Mission Delta 4 continues the missile-warning mission on SBIRS while preparing for the first Next-Gen OPIR GEO satellite, part of a multi-orbit architecture that adds proliferated MEO (SSC) and LEO (SDA) layers. Another visible example of legacy-to-new-architecture transition, alongside the A-10 retirement covered last week.

Sept 29Guetlein tells Senate early Golden Dome capability possible by 2028; continuing resolution is the risk

Gen. Michael Guetlein testified to the Senate Strategic Forces Subcommittee that an early version of Golden Dome could be operating in summer 2028, but warned a continuing resolution doesn't work for the program: it was funded through reconciliation, so there is no prior-year top line for a CR to carry forward. Reporting says the CR, running through Dec. 11, also blocks new starts across 150+ Pentagon programs. Corroborated across Air & Space Forces Magazine, Payload and Industrial Base Alpha; the exact hearing date (Sept 29) comes from a single outlet's summary, so treat it as likely rather than settled.

Air & Space Forces Magazine · Payload
Reported Oct 1–2Air Force and Space Force meet FY2026 recruiting goals; FY2027 targets raised

The Department of the Air Force enlisted 31,950 Airmen and 730 Guardians in FY2026 (the April “goal met early” figure of 32,750 Airmen was later adjusted for retention and training-date availability). FY2027 targets are 36,300 Airmen and 1,402 Guardians — nearly double the Guardian goal, a signal of Space Force growth that carries IT, training and infrastructure demand.
