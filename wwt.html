Early NovSchiess: FY27 growth of 2,800 Guardians and 2,000 civilians would go largely to components, if funded

Gen. Schiess said the Space Force wants to add 2,800 Guardians and 2,000 civilians in FY2027 if the budget comes through, with "a lot of that" going to the service components supporting combatant commands (CENTCOM, EUCOM, NORTHCOM, AFRICOM, INDOPACOM). Reported by both Air & Space Forces Magazine and Federal News Network. One search summary dates the remarks to Nov 4, but that exact date couldn't be confirmed from a second source, so treat it as early November. Federal News Network separately reports that the service wants civilians to adopt the Guardian identity, adding training and weighing a new civilian-advocacy role on the Department of the Air Force secretariat staff. Related: the Space Force component to SOCOM (SPACEFOR-SOC) has been activated at MacDill, but sources disagree on the date (Oct 7 vs. Nov 2), so the date is unconfirmed.

The Space Force growth Schiess described above, and the push for its components, depends on that outcome. Inference only; nothing from WWT addresses the CR directly.
