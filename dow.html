Sep 16DISA drops first of two JWCC cloud follow-on solicitations: $21.6B ceiling, bids due Oct 6

DISA released the final RFP for JWCC Unified Cloud Marketplace ("UCM") Core — a hyperscaler-focused IDIQ worth up to $21.6B over a 5-year base plus a 5-year option, covering IaaS/PaaS/SaaS (DISA's own phrase: "anything-as-a-service"). A companion "UCM Premier" vehicle for non-hyperscaler and AI-heavy vendors is still being built separately. Translation: if you're wrapping managed services or integration around a hyperscaler's cloud, Core is your lane; if you're pitching something more specialized, watch for Premier. Questions were due Sept 11; bids close Oct 6.

Washington Technology · GovConWire
Sep 16Air Force stands up "Megatron" IDIQ to fast-track Battle Network buys

The Air Force is establishing a new IDIQ vehicle nicknamed "Megatron" specifically to speed up acquisition for the Department of the Air Force's Battle Network — the C2/sensor-fusion architecture that underpins JADC2 for the service. Early-stage, but worth tracking as a potential faster door into DAF network-modernization dollars than the standard IDIQ crawl.

DefenseScoop
Sep 11–17Pentagon ~90% through purging Anthropic from classified AI workloads; deadline end of September

DefenseScoop reports the Pentagon is close to finishing its migration of classified-network AI workloads off Anthropic's Claude models entirely, following DoD's formal "supply-chain risk" designation after Anthropic declined to permit "all lawful" government use cases (which Anthropic said could include autonomous weapons and domestic surveillance). Eight vendors — reportedly including OpenAI and Google — have signed on as classified-network replacements. If you're positioning any AI capability for DoD/DOW classified environments, the vendor eligibility list just got a lot more consequential.

DefenseScoop
Sep 15Space Force creates new office to accelerate innovative tech acquisition

USSF stood up a dedicated office aimed at speeding the path from prototype to program of record for innovative technology, echoing the Air Force's Megatron move above — a service-wide push to shorten acquisition timelines rather than a single-program fix.

DefenseScoop
Sep 16Lawmakers warn AI-enabled targeting may outpace human authentication

At a Wednesday Washington hearing, critics told lawmakers that AI-assisted military targeting systems risk moving faster than humans can verify targets — a live policy fight with direct implications for any C2/JADC2 or AI-decision-support capability you're selling into USAF or USSF, since it's the kind of concern that tends to show up in acquisition requirements a budget cycle or two later.
