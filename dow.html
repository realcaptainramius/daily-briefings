Oct 27Space Force plans up to $945.9M sole-source IDIQ to Boeing for WGS/UFO satellite communications sustainment

Defense Daily, GovConWire and Washington Technology report Space Systems Command posted a notice of intent for a sole-source IDIQ to Boeing: a five-year base ordering period valued at $480.6 million plus a 10-year option worth $465.3 million. Scope covers life-cycle engineering, sustainment, cybersecurity, modernization and operations support for the WGS and UFO satellite networks. The notice says the Space Force lacks the data rights to let others do the work and that Boeing has declined to sell them. Responses are due Nov 17; this is a planned award, not a final one. Notice date (Oct 27 per one outlet) was seen via search summaries only.

Oct 27Lockheed Martin wins about $694M Air Force contract for ARRW hypersonic missile production

A Lockheed Martin release (reprinted by Joint Forces) and a Defense Department contract announcement dated Oct 27 say Lockheed Martin received about $694 million for production of the Air-Launched Rapid Response Weapon (ARRW), a hypersonic missile integrated on the B-52. Lockheed says it has already funded materials and equipment to start production. Only reprints and search summaries were seen, so contract type, period and obligated amount are unconfirmed. This is a weapons award rather than IT/network work.
