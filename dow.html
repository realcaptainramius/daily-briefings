Sep 15 (released)DISA drops first JWCC follow-on solicitation: $21.6B hyperscaler cloud vehicle

DISA released "JWCC Unified Cloud Marketplace Core," the hyperscaler-focused half of the Joint Warfighter Cloud Capability recompete — a 10-year, $21.6B ceiling IDIQ spanning IaaS/PaaS/SaaS, with bids due noon Eastern on Oct 6. A companion "UCM Premier" vehicle for non-hyperscale and small-business providers is still coming, expected to release in Q1 2027. Translation: if you sell cloud-adjacent infrastructure or integration work into DoD, the hyperscaler lane closes fast (three weeks) but the door for everyone else stays open a while longer — plan your positioning accordingly.

DefenseScoop · Defense One
Sep 13–15Pentagon CIO: "we have to throw technology at the cyber problem"

DoD CIO Kirsten Davies, speaking at the Billington CyberSecurity Summit, said the department can't keep throwing people at its cyber staffing gap and needs to lean harder on automation, machine learning, and AI to manage what she called "a complex Rubik's cube" of legacy and modern IT systems. Comes as the Pentagon's first overarching cyber strategy since 2023 is reportedly due out as soon as this week, expected to center on cross-domain integration, forcing an adversary advantage, and reorganizing cyber forces. Worth watching — a CIO publicly saying "we need more tech, not more people" is the kind of talking point that opens doors for automation and AI-assisted cyber tooling.
