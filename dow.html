Sep 23Space Force leads first Guardian-led human-AI command-and-control experiment (DASH-4)

The fourth iteration of the "Decision Advantage Sprint for Human-Machine Teaming" ran as a two-week exercise at USAF's Shadow Operations Center-Nellis — the first DASH iteration led by a Guardian, pairing a Space Force-led integrated crew with Air Force battle managers and industry software developers to validate AI-assisted C2 tools. The ABMS Cross-Functional Team and Air Force Research Laboratory co-led the effort, with the 711th Human Performance Wing tracking decision quality and physiological data across scenarios.

Sep 23Viasat/Inmarsat Government wins first task order under $307M Marine Corps SATCOM IDIQ

Viasat, through subsidiary Inmarsat Government, was awarded the Marine Enterprise Commercial Satcom Satellite Services (MECS2) contract by the Space Force's Commercial Satellite Communications Office: a 7-year IDIQ worth up to $307M, with an initial $42M task order. Viasat will provide fully managed, multi-orbit (L/Ku/Ka-band) satellite services and secure terrestrial data transport for the Marine Corps.

Sep 23Pentagon: GenAI.mil hits roughly 500,000 power users

At the DefenseTalks conference, a senior Pentagon official said roughly 500,000 of the 1.7 million personnel with GenAI.mil access are now heavy users of the platform, which offers Gemini, ChatGPT and Grok and has been adopted enterprise-wide including by the Air Force and Space Force. CDAO Cameron Stanley flagged compute capacity as the next constraint as the department shifts focus to agentic-workflow integration.

Sep 23Space Force gives SpaceX access to classified space-tracking catalog in data-sharing deal

The Space Force's Joint Commercial Operations Cell signed an agreement giving SpaceX access to U.S. Space Command's most detailed classified catalog of on-orbit objects (the High Accuracy Catalog), in exchange for SpaceX sharing data from its "Stargaze" star-tracking cameras. Competing commercial space-tracking firms have raised concerns the arrangement gives SpaceX an unfair data advantage.
