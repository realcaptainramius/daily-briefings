Sep 18–21Space Force begins market research for enterprise SATCOM integration contract

Via a Sept 18 sources-sought notice, Space Force started scoping a potential five-year system-integrator contract to plan, execute and validate integrations between its enterprise SATCOM management platform (which runs on a Microsoft Azure-based classified staging/production environment) and other government systems — the Joint Management Tool, Unified Data Library, EMS Beskar, Blitzar, and the Protected and Tactical Systems Mission Management System. The platform covers satellite mission planning, resource management, EMI tracking, and terminal/service-provider management. Early-stage market research, not yet a formal solicitation, but worth tracking for SATCOM integration and cloud-adjacent DoD work.

Sep 21Northrop Grumman and True Anomaly picked for GHOST-R reconnaissance-satellite program

The Pentagon tapped Northrop Grumman and True Anomaly to build satellites able to monitor other space systems under the GHOST-R initiative. Contract value undisclosed; Northrop, the Space Systems Command, and True Anomaly did not respond to requests for dollar figures. Space-domain-awareness angle rather than comms/network, but relevant given the same players' SATCOM work.
