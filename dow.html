Oct 5–10SDA’s first Northrop Grumman Transport Layer plane (T1TL-A) re-set for Oct 10 after Oct 5 abort (launch reported by secondary outlets)

The fourth Tranche 1 mission, 21 Northrop Grumman-built data transport satellites on a Falcon 9 from Vandenberg SLC-4E, auto-aborted with about 58 seconds left on Oct 5 (cause undisclosed; SpaceX said vehicle and payload were healthy). Spaceflight Now (Oct 9) reported a retry set for 12:39 a.m. PDT Saturday Oct 10. The satellites reached Vandenberg Sept 28 and SDA expects Tranche 1 to begin providing operational capability in 2027. Secondary outlets (tech-insider.org, Neoteo, Basenor) report liftoff at 12:39 a.m. PDT Oct 10 with booster B1103 landing at LZ-4; no post-launch SDA or SpaceX statement or Spaceflight Now/SpaceNews write-up was found, so treat it as reported but not confirmed by a primary source.
