Sep 15SDA lobbies to keep its acquisition authorities as Congress debates dissolving it

With lawmakers still weighing draft NDAA language that would fold the Space Development Agency into the Space Force (or eliminate it outright, per earlier House Armed Services drafts), SDA leadership is pushing to retain certain decision-making authorities even under a reorg — while staff keep grinding on near-term deadlines to launch the next Proliferated Warfighter Space Architecture (PWSA) tranche. Translation: if you sell into SDA's data-transport or missile-tracking layer, the org chart you're navigating in six months may not be the one you're navigating today. Worth tracking closely rather than assuming continuity.

Sep 15–16New Space Force chief: CR will stall 1 in 10 programs

Gen. Doug Schiess, sworn in Sept 3 as the service's third Chief of Space Operations succeeding Gen. Chance Saltzman, said the continuing resolution now funding the government through Dec 11 will hold up roughly 10% of Space Force programs. No full FY27 budget deal is expected anytime soon. Worth flagging for anyone with a fiscal-year-dependent SATCOM or comms proposal in the pipeline — a long CR tends to freeze new starts before it freezes sustainment.
