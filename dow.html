Sep 19Space Force to prototype software fusing commercial and military space-tracking data

Col. Barry Croker of Mission Delta 2 told Breaking Defense at the AMOS conference in Maui that Space Force plans a prototype environment, targeted for later this fall, to automatically pull commercial space-tracking feeds (currently siloed in a separate cloud via the Joint Commercial Operations cell) together with military sensor data. The design deliberately trades precision for speed — accepting lower-fidelity commercial reads to get faster warning on new debris clouds and freshly launched satellite clusters. Translation: if you sell into space domain awareness or commercial SSA data feeds, this MVP-first prototype is worth tracking as an early on-ramp.

Breaking Defense · KeepTrack Space Brief
Sep 17First Space-Based AMTI prototypes launching this month; parallel Ground MTI effort named "Resilient Radar System-Ground"

SecAF Troy Meink said at AFA's conference that the first Space-Based Airborne Moving Target Indicator (SB-AMTI) satellites — a $4.16B SpaceX-led constellation meant to track aircraft, drones and missiles from orbit as adversary air defenses push crewed ISR platforms further back — will launch before month's end. Space Force is separately working with Northrop Grumman toward an on-orbit Ground MTI constellation ("Resilient Radar System-Ground") within two years, co-developed with the NRO. Neither is comms/network per se, but both feed the same battle-management/C2 data pipes your SATCOM and network customers care about.
