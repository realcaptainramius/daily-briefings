Sep 28Hegseth memo directs Cyber Command, NSA, DIA and NGA to defend 2026 elections from foreign interference

The Pentagon released a Sept 22 memo from Secretary of War Pete Hegseth directing U.S. Cyber Command and combat support agencies to “deploy advanced intelligence and cyber capabilities” to identify, disrupt and neutralize foreign interference in U.S. elections, in coordination with DHS. The memo, addressed to the Cyber Command chief and the directors of NSA, DIA and NGA, relies on existing authorities and announced no new capabilities, staffing or funding.
