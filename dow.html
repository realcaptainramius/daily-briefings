Sep 24–28Space Force and MDA reset SCAR satellite-control antenna program around Commercial Solutions Openings

The Space Force and Missile Defense Agency announced a restructured Satellite Communications Augmentation Resource (SCAR) program, the effort to revitalize the Cold War-era, 19-antenna Satellite Control Network. Rather than a single buy, SCAR will run phased Commercial Solutions Openings covering government-owned/contractor-operated commercial TT&C, exclusive-use leased antenna capacity, and antenna-as-a-service on shared commercial ground networks. Industry white papers are due October 15, 2026. The notice was dated Sept 24; trade coverage ran Sept 28.
