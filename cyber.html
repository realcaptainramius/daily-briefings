Breaches & Incidents

Nov 14La Poste confirms Maileva breach exposed names and postal addresses of 22 million people

La Poste confirmed to AFP on Nov 14 that an intrusion at its mail-services subsidiary Maileva, dating to mid-September, exposed the first names, surnames and postal addresses of about 22 million people who receive mail sent through Maileva by its corporate clients. Reports say attackers may also have reached digital copies of some letters sent by clients such as hospitals and employers, and that several thousand corporate clients were affected; La Poste does not describe it as an account takeover. The Paris prosecutor’s cybercrime unit is reportedly investigating. A figure of about 39 million email references comes from a single outlet and is unverified. Seen via search summaries of press coverage only.

Nov 11–12Attackers abuse cloud ScreenConnect at MSPs to push “SCKit” backdoor to 288 endpoints

Huntress reports that on Nov 11 attackers used ScreenConnect’s Run Command feature in cloud-hosted instances at six or more managed service providers to push rogue clients and an “SCKit” backdoor toolkit to 288 endpoints across 48 organizations within 90 minutes. The likely entry point was RMM+, a third-party ScreenConnect extension, and ConnectWise disabled five extensions across about 400 partner environments. Some outlets call SCKit worm-like, but Huntress and Blackpoint did not see it spread successfully to patched clients. Seen via search summaries.

Vulnerabilities & Exploits

Nov 13CISA adds exploited UnrealIRCd and Check Point flaws to KEV

CISA added two flaws to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on Nov 13: an UnrealIRCd improper-neutralization bug (CVE-2026-114713) and a Check Point “multiple products” incorrect-permission-assignment flaw (CVE-2026-123039). The UnrealIRCd bug is an escaping flaw in the IRCv3 labeled-response module that can let ordinary users on linked-server networks inject server-level commands. The project called it the most critical issue in its 15-year history and reported in-the-wild exploitation; it is fixed in 6.2.7.1 or a no-restart hot-patch. No Check Point advisory or details for CVE-2026-123039 were found, so the affected products and exploitation context are unconfirmed. No KEV additions dated Nov 14–16 were found. Seen via search summaries; the CISA alert itself could not be opened.

Nov 8–12N-able N-central zero-day exploited within hours of public PoC, even on latest hotfix

BleepingComputer reports researcher Sean Bales released “4EverAble2Shell” and “Agent2Shell” exploits claiming root on N-central appliances and SYSTEM on Windows agents. Patch and CVE status for the new flaw is unconfirmed. Seen via search summaries only.
