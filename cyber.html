Vulnerabilities & Exploits

Oct 28Attackers reportedly scanning for recently patched Oracle E-Business Suite flaw (CVE-2026-101767)

BleepingComputer reports, per a search summary, that threat intelligence firm Defused saw attackers scanning the internet for systems vulnerable to CVE-2026-101767 in Oracle E-Business Suite. Oracle had not tagged the flaw as exploited in the wild at the time. Severity and affected versions were not seen.

BleepingComputer
Oct 27Patches: Tailscale SSH flaws, Brotli decoder bug, HPE Aruba, Microsoft eMudhra certificate update

Search-summary listings from BleepingComputer and SecurityWeek on Oct 27 mention Tailscale patching almost a dozen flaws, including SSH issues that could allow root privilege escalation; a Brotli decoder flaw prompting patches across Microsoft, Node.js and Python packages that can lead to code execution; HPE Aruba fixing dozens of flaws including eight max-severity ones; and Microsoft out-of-band Windows updates for certificates tied to compromised eMudhra certificate authority keys. CVE IDs, scores and exploitation status were not seen, and the SecurityWeek page mixed in older headlines, so dates are unconfirmed.

BleepingComputer · SecurityWeek
Oct 27CISA reportedly adds “Node2Shell” Brotli decoder flaw to KEV

Secondary coverage (Cyberpress, a threat-report aggregator and, per a search summary, SecurityWeek) says CISA added CVE-2026-111497, dubbed Node2Shell, on Oct 27 with a Nov 10 deadline for federal civilian agencies. It reportedly affects Google’s Brotli decoder, including copies bundled with Node.js, and ties to the Brotli patches noted above. CISA’s own alert was not found, so the date, CVE and deadline are unconfirmed.

Oct 26CISA adds two PHP flaws (“PHP2Shell”) to KEV

CISA added CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619, two PHP flaws that can allow unauthenticated remote code execution under high-complexity conditions, to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, with a federal patch deadline reported as Thursday (Oct 29). Coverage says the PHP team patched them Oct 21 and that Amazon Threat Intelligence saw attackers targeting them four days before the patches. Details come from search summaries; the CISA alert text was not reviewed.
