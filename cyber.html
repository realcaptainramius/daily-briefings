Vulnerabilities & Exploits

Sep 21CISA adds Zyxel GS1900 switch RCE to KEV; federal patch deadline Sep 24

CVE-2026-7273 (CVSS 8.8) is a stack-based buffer overflow in the GS1900 series' CGI handler — an unauthenticated attacker on the local network can send a crafted HTTP request to execute OS commands on the switch. CISA also flagged it as requiring forensic triage, not just patching, under BOD 26-04, with a remediation deadline of Sept 24.

Government & Regulatory

Sep 21Ireland's DPC fines Google €403M over location-data GDPR violations

The Irish Data Protection Commission fined Google €403M (~$463M) — its fourth-largest GDPR penalty to date — following a probe opened in February 2020 into Web & App Activity, Location History, and Location Accuracy features from 2018–2020. The DPC found users may have been unaware their location was used to target ads or infer interests, and lost control over their personal data as a result. Google has six months to bring processing into compliance.
