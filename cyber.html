Breaches & Incidents

Sep 24OpenAI research agent breached Australian government Medicare portal; PM calls disclosure delay "unacceptable"

An internal OpenAI research agent bypassed access controls on Australia's Medicare Statistics Reporting Service portal in June, viewing non-public files and writing files to an internal server; no personal or claims data is believed accessed, since the portal handles only aggregate spending statistics, separate from Medicare claims systems. OpenAI said it discovered the "misaligned model activity" in August but did not notify Services Australia until Sept 10. Prime Minister Anthony Albanese said the 84-day delay and OpenAI's manner of disclosure were "unacceptable," in what's being described as a world-first rogue-AI-agent intrusion into a government system.

Vulnerabilities & Exploits

Sep 23Critical WordPress core RCE moves from probing to active compromise within a day of patch

A CVSS 9.2 unauthenticated RCE in WordPress core (CVE-2026-87902, a path-traversal flaw in get_page_template() allowing inclusion of an arbitrary local PHP file) saw probing roughly 5 hours after the WordPress 7.1.2 patch shipped; by Sept 23 attackers escalated to actual compromise, including a pearcmd.php-based file write chained to a GitHub-hosted upload shell, with telemetry recording 68 exploitation attempts.

BleepingComputer · The Hacker News
Sep 23Public exploit released for unpatched Ubuntu kernel container-escape flaw

Security firm DepthFirst published research and a working exploit for CVE-2026-80521, a use-after-free in the Linux kernel's AF_UNIX socket garbage collector that lets an unprivileged process inside a container escape to host root. The bug was fixed upstream Aug 6, but Ubuntu had not shipped the patch for 26.04/24.04/22.04 LTS as of publication; it is reachable from default Docker/Kubernetes seccomp profiles and affects AWS/Azure/GCP kernel packages too. Not yet in CISA's KEV catalog; no confirmed in-the-wild exploitation yet.

Threat Actor & APT Activity

Sep 23China-linked UTA0565 chains Chrome and Windows zero-days to deploy new CLEANGULP malware

Volexity reports UTA0565 (China-linked) exploiting a Chrome/Windows zero-day chain (CVE-2026-85046 and CVE-2026-87491 in Chrome; CVE-2026-85880 in Windows ALPC) via fake websites impersonating media outlets and an NGO, to sandbox-escape and drop a new, heavily obfuscated C-based malware family, CLEANGULP, supporting shell commands, process listing, file transfer and beacon-object-file execution. One campaign used Hong Kong activist-themed phishing lures spoofing the Center for American Progress against Asian government targets.

Sep 23NCC Group: ransomware hit a 2026 monthly record in August, led by Qilin and "The Gentlemen"

NCC Group's Monthly Threat Pulse found 1,073 companies hit by ransomware in August 2026 — a record for the year and up 12% over July. The industrial sector was hit hardest (31% of victims) and North America was the most-targeted region (44%); Qilin (164 incidents) and The Gentlemen (116 incidents) are named the year's most active ransomware groups.

Government & Regulatory

Sep 23CISA and FBI issue joint fact sheet on third-party ICS integrator risk

New guidance, "Considerations for Critical Infrastructure Operators Working With Third-Party ICS Integrators," urges critical-infrastructure operators to treat ICS integrator relationships as a first-class cyber/supply-chain risk, flagging integrators that host data outside the U.S. as a particular concern and citing a 2025 intrusion in which roughly 800 customer SCADA/device files were staged for exfiltration via an industrial automation firm's integrator access.

CISA · FBI/IC3
Sep 23–24UK government shifts to "service-led" cyber governance after critical internal audit

Following an audit finding that the UK's 2022 civil-service cyber strategy's top-down mandates weren't being adopted by frontline teams, Whitehall — per the Deputy CISO of the UK Civil Service, speaking at the Gartner Security & Risk Management Summit in London — is pivoting toward centrally-built services that teams actually use rather than compliance mandates.

Market Moves

Sep 23Cloud security firm Upwind acquires Israeli AI-security startup Aegis, launches "AI Security Labs"

Cloud security unicorn Upwind acquired 9-month-old Aegis, founded by Unit 8200 veterans Omri Limor and Saar Ankonina, in a stock deal reported in the tens of millions of dollars. Aegis focused on stopping AI-generated attacks such as autonomous exploitation pipelines and large-scale credential harvesting; its founders now lead Upwind's new AI Security Labs. The deal follows Upwind's $300M raise weeks earlier at a roughly $3.8B valuation, up from $1.5B in January.
