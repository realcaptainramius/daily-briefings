Vulnerabilities & Exploits

Oct 26CISA adds two PHP flaws (“PHP2Shell”) to KEV

CISA added CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619, two PHP flaws that can allow unauthenticated remote code execution under high-complexity conditions, to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, with a federal patch deadline reported as Thursday (Oct 29). Coverage says the PHP team patched them Oct 21 and that Amazon Threat Intelligence saw attackers targeting them four days before the patches. Details come from search summaries; the CISA alert text was not reviewed.
