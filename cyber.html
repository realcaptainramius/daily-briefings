Breaches & Incidents

Sep 25Arizona state court system breached, exposing protective-order addresses

The Arizona Supreme Court/Administrative Office of the Courts disclosed that hackers breached the state court system and copied personally identifiable information on many Arizonans. Most sensitive: confidential home addresses of people with current or former protective orders may have been exposed, raising safety concerns for domestic-violence survivors. No evidence yet that stolen data has been shared publicly; the chief justice is coordinating with the FBI's Arizona field office.

Vulnerabilities & Exploits

Sep 27Citrix confirms two actively exploited NetScaler zero-days; CISA orders federal patching by Sept 30

Citrix confirmed active in-the-wild exploitation of CVE-2026-88771 (CVSS 9.5, improper input validation allowing unauthenticated arbitrary command execution on any NetScaler ADC/Gateway build, no special feature required) and CVE-2026-88772 (memory overflow leading to RCE/DoS when DTLS is enabled, on by default for VPN virtual servers). CISA added both to its KEV catalog the same day, confirming global exploitation and giving federal civilian agencies until Sept 30 to patch under BOD 26-04. Fixes ship in NetScaler ADC/Gateway 14.1-73.37 and 13.1-64.23, alongside six other patched flaws (CVE-2026-88773 through -88778).

Sep 25Teen researcher discloses Microsoft internal "Titan" analytics auth bypass

A 16-year-old researcher publicly detailed an authentication flaw in Microsoft's internal Titan analytics service, where unsigned JWTs allowed forged admin access and raw SQL queries against a metadata-estimated 17.3 trillion database rows (not confirmed unique customer records). Reported to MSRC Sept 5, locked down by Microsoft Sept 9, a $5,000 bounty paid Sept 17; no evidence of malicious exploitation.

Sep 24–25Suspected North Korean hackers steal $351.6M from crypto exchange Bitget

Bitget said attackers compromised a backend wallet system, spoofed transaction data and drained hot/warm wallets of $351.6M across Ethereum, XRP Ledger, Arbitrum, Avalanche, Optimism, BSC and Base, with unauthorized transfers first identified at 18:31 UTC Sept 24. Bitget CEO Gracy Chen linked the attack pattern and VPN infrastructure to known North Korean tradecraft; Mandiant and SlowMist are investigating. The theft pushes North Korea's 2026 confirmed/suspected crypto haul past $1.04B, the second-largest year on record and the largest single heist of 2026. Losses will be covered by Bitget's $464M+ User Protection Fund.

Cloud & Infrastructure Security

Sep 25Microsoft details AI-driven "Storm-3168" Azure attack that destroyed cloud resources via a leaked secret

Microsoft published a new report on a June 2026 attack, publicly detailed for the first time Sept 25, in which two compromised Azure service principals mapped a victim tenant and, in about 18 hours (roughly 7 minutes for the destructive burst), deleted 100+ storage accounts, a Key Vault and a Function App, and pulled storage account keys. Root cause: a service principal's client ID/secret/tenant ID was pasted into a public GitHub issue and remained visible in the issue's public edit history even after the post was edited. Microsoft links the activity to JADEPUFFER, previously described as the first ransomware operation run end-to-end with LLM assistance.
