Vulnerabilities & Exploits

Oct 29–Nov 2Ivanti patches two more Connect Secure flaws chained in zero-day attacks; CISA deadlines reported

BleepingComputer reports Ivanti patched two further Connect Secure flaws that attackers chained in zero-day attacks, saying exploitation began at least Oct 1. A secondary write-up (tech-insider) names CVE-2026-111357 and CVE-2026-118527, dates the patch Oct 29, and says federal agencies had until Nov 2 and have until Nov 13 respectively under CISA KEV; it conflicts with the earlier Oct 30 KEV report below on timing. Seen via search summaries only; Ivanti and CISA advisories were not reviewed.

BleepingComputer · tech-insider
Oct 30CISA reportedly adds two Ivanti Connect Secure flaws to KEV; Node-RED admin API flaw exploited

Search summaries say CISA added two Ivanti Connect Secure flaws to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on Oct 30 and that attackers were reported to chain three Ivanti flaws for unauthenticated root access; SecurityWeek lists related Ivanti patches under Nov 2. Separately, VulnCheck says one of its canaries caught an attacker exploiting CVE-2026-58115, an unauthenticated Node-RED admin API flaw, on Oct 30. CVE IDs for the Ivanti flaws, deadlines and the chain details were not seen, and none of the advisories was reviewed.

VulnCheck · SecurityWeek
Oct 29–30CISA adds max-severity GitLab flaw CVE-2026-97229 to KEV; Cisco ASA/FTD VPN zero-day CVE-2026-76418 also listed

BleepingComputer and Security Affairs report CISA confirmed exploitation of CVE-2026-97229, a CVSS 10.0 GitLab remote code execution flaw triggered by a crafted regular expression in CI/CD configuration, fixed in GitLab 19.4.2, 19.3.x and 19.2.8 (sources disagree on the 19.3 point release). Most sources date the KEV addition Oct 29 with a federal deadline of Nov 1; some coverage is dated Oct 30. Shadowserver tracks nearly 2,800 exposed GitLab instances. Separately, Cisco patched CVE-2026-76418 (CVSS 8.6), an ASA/FTD SSL VPN flaw that lets an unauthenticated attacker force a reload and was exploited in the wild, also reported added to KEV Oct 29; patch dates differ (Oct 28 or 29) and Cisco had published no indicators of compromise as of Oct 30. Details are from search summaries; the CISA and vendor advisories were not reviewed.
