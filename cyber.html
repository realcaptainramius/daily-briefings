Breaches & Incidents

Sep 3–17ShinyHunters breaches Florida DMV "DAVID" database via stolen police credentials

ShinyHunters claims it stole 200,000+ driver records from Florida's DAVID system after compromising accounts — including one belonging to an FBI agent — using credentials improperly stored on a Plant City Police Department employee's personal device. The breach began Sept 3; Florida's DMV (FLHSMV) confirmed and says it was mitigated. The gang has been posting proof on its leak site, including data on a high-profile deceased individual, to pressure a ransom.

Vulnerabilities & Exploits

Sep 15–16Ransomware gangs now confirmed exploiting critical VMware vCenter RCE (CVSS 9.8) across 47 countries

CISA says ransomware operators have moved from establishing persistence to active exploitation of CVE-2026-59310, an unauthenticated directory-traversal RCE in vCenter's Syslog server (patched by Broadcom July 29). DFIR firm QUIRSO found 361+ compromised IPs across 47 countries, with attackers deploying reverse-SSH tools for persistent access. CISA added it to KEV with a 3-day federal patch window — treat any internet-facing vCenter as compromised until verified otherwise.

BleepingComputer · Petri
Sep 15–16Google patches zero-click Pixel modem flaw exploited in targeted attacks

CVE-2026-58704 is a permission-bypass logic error in the Pixel cellular modem enabling remote privilege escalation with no user interaction — a zero-click bug, and the "limited, targeted" framing plus modem-level exploitation point toward spyware-vendor or state-actor tradecraft. Google patched it Sept 15; CISA added it to KEV Sept 16 with a Sept 19 FCEB deadline.

BleepingComputer · The Register
Sep 16Acronis Backup plugin for cPanel/WHM exploited for Linux privilege escalation

CVE-2026-87886 (CVSS 7.8) stems from insecure default file permissions in Acronis's Backup plugin for cPanel and WHM, letting a low-privileged local attacker escalate to root. Acronis confirms limited, targeted exploitation but hasn't disclosed post-exploitation payloads. Added to CISA's KEV catalog Sept 16, Sept 19 FCEB deadline.

Help Net Security · The Hacker News
Sep 15–17Two critical Cisco bugs (ISE, Secure Email Gateway) remain under active exploitation; Sept 17 patch deadline now passed

Follow-up on yesterday's items: CVE-2026-76460 (Cisco ISE, CVSS 10.0, unauthenticated root) and CVE-2026-76461 (Cisco Secure Email Gateway, root command execution) carried a CISA-mandated Sept 17 patch deadline for federal agencies. If you haven't confirmed patching on either product, treat this as overdue, not routine.

APT28 (aka UAC-0001, Fancy Bear) has been attributed to attacks exploiting a newly disclosed Microsoft Office vulnerability as part of a campaign dubbed Operation Neusploit — another reminder that patch-cycle SLAs for Office/Windows flaws now need to assume state-actor weaponization within days, not weeks.

German IR firm QUIRSO's investigation into the vCenter CVE-2026-59310 exploitation (see Vulnerabilities above) traces early activity to a China-nexus APT establishing persistence shortly after the July patch, before ransomware crews piled on this month — a useful reminder that KEV entries often understate how long sophisticated actors have already had a foothold.

Government & Regulatory

Sep 16–19CISA loads three new actively-exploited CVEs into KEV with a common Sept 19 deadline

The Pixel modem flaw, the Acronis Backup plugin bug, and the VMware vCenter RCE all landed in CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog within a day of each other, all carrying a Sept 19 FCEB patch deadline under Binding Operational Directive 22-01. Three unrelated products, one compressed patch window — worth flagging to any customer running Pixel fleets, cPanel/WHM with Acronis, or vCenter.
