Vulnerabilities & Exploits

Oct 14CISA adds four exploited flaws to KEV: PAN-OS, Cisco Secure Email Gateway and both AhsayCBS bugs

CISA’s Oct 14 alert lists CVE-2026-0322 (Palo Alto Networks PAN-OS code injection), CVE-2026-76519 (Cisco Secure Email Gateway path traversal), and the two AhsayCBS flaws covered below, CVE-2026-105133 and CVE-2026-105134. Federal civilian agencies have until Oct 17 to remediate. Help Net Security reported the additions on Oct 15. The PAN-OS and Cisco entries come from the CISA alert title and search summaries; vendor advisories, CVSS scores and exploitation details for those two were not reviewed.

CISA · Help Net Security
Oct 13Ivanti Connect Secure authentication-bypass zero-day CVE-2026-104935 exploited; CISA adds to KEV

Ivanti disclosed CVE-2026-104935 (CVSS 8.6) on Oct 13 and confirmed exploitation of a limited number of customers before disclosure. Volexity, which found the activity, tracks the actor as UTA0576 and says it chained the flaw since at least Oct 3 with other bugs to get root-level code execution on fully patched appliances, leaving no malware or web shell and tampering with logs. Fixes cited: 22.8R2.5 and 25.1.1.2, plus 25.1.3.0/25.1.3.1; sources differ on the affected-version list and on whether a privilege-escalation fix is still pending. Volexity assessed with low confidence that the motive is criminal rather than espionage. CISA added it to KEV Oct 13 and urged immediate updates. Because logs may be altered, check appliance integrity as well as logs.

Rapid7 · BleepingComputer · The Hacker News · CISA
Oct 13Microsoft’s record October Patch Tuesday fixes 1,400+ CVEs; Windows Shell zero-day CVE-2026-96951 exploited

BleepingComputer’s headline counts 1,411 flaws and three zero-days; other outlets count 1,407 to 1,413, and some report only one actively exploited. The exploited one, CVE-2026-96951 (CVSS 8.8), is a Windows Shell command-injection flaw that bypasses a security feature (Mark of the Web per BleepingComputer). CISA added it to KEV Oct 13 with a federal deadline of Oct 16; Microsoft has not said who is exploiting it. Two other zero-days were publicly disclosed, in .NET Framework-related components. Dark Reading also reports Outlook.com was affected but already patched, from a single source. One outlet lists CVE-2026-92707 as exploited, which other reports do not support.

BleepingComputer · Rapid7 · Dark Reading · CISA
Oct 7–9Attackers chain AhsayCBS flaws CVE-2026-105133/105134 to drop web shells and XMRig miners

Huntress reports exploitation beginning Oct 7 (23:20 UTC) and five organizations targeted as of Oct 8. CVE-2026-105133 (CVSS v4 5.5) is an improper-authentication flaw and CVE-2026-105134 (CVSS v4 9.3) an OS command injection in the Replication Receiver; chained, they allow authentication bypass and command execution. Attackers ran reconnaissance, dropped JSP web shells and a miner disguised as edge.exe persisting as “MicrosoftEdgeUpdateSvc”. Patch status is disputed: Huntress first said 10.3.4 was unaffected, then reportedly found 10.3.4 (the latest version) also affected, so restrict management-interface access until Ahsay confirms a fix.

Oct 9Seven-nation advisory on China-linked Flax Typhoon; CISA adds five exploited flaws to KEV

The Hacker News reports CISA added five older flaws to KEV after exploitation by Flax Typhoon: ProFTPD CVE-2015-3306 (CVSS 10.0), ONLYOFFICE Docs CVE-2021-3199 (9.8), Strapi CVE-2023-22894 (7.2), Apache Struts CVE-2016-3081 (8.1) and ISC BIND CVE-2015-5477 (7.5). The additions coincide with a joint advisory from seven countries that links the activity to China-based Integrity Technology Group and covers eight vulnerabilities in total. The headline cites an Oct 11 federal deadline, which was not verified against CISA’s KEV entries. VulnCheck separately says a Flax Typhoon-linked botnet targets 66 flaws, only 27 of them in KEV.
