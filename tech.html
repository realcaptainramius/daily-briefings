Post-Quantum Cryptography

NIST/NSA standards, CNSA 2.0 and CMMC deadlines, and PQC vendor/DoD adoption.

UpcomingCivilian-agency PQC migration plans due to OMB/ONCD around October 22

Per secondary reporting, OMB Memorandum M-26-15 (issued June 24, following Executive Order 14412 of June 22) requires each executive department and agency to submit a PQC migration plan to OMB and the Office of the National Cyber Director within 120 days — roughly October 22, 2026. Plans are to cover risk-based prioritization, phased timelines, automated cryptographic inventory, crypto-agility architecture, third-party coordination, and funding. The memo does not apply to National Security Systems, which stay on the CNSA 2.0 track below. Unconfirmed against the memo text itself: all sources found are vendor/consultancy blogs and cite the date as approximate, so verify before relying on the exact day. Expect civilian-agency customers to start asking vendors for crypto inventories and PQC roadmaps in the run-up. No new NIST, NSA, or CISA PQC publications were verified for Oct 5–12; NSA's Oct 1 PQC Resource Hub announcement (covered last week) remains the latest.

Secureframe · ISI Defense
OngoingCNSA 2.0: network gear now, every new NSS acquisition by Jan 1, 2027

NSA's CNSA 2.0 suite (ML-KEM-1024 for key establishment, ML-DSA-87 for signatures, plus AES-256/SHA-384-512) remains the mandated quantum-resistant algorithm set for National Security Systems. VPNs, firewalls, routers, and switches used in NSS need CNSA 2.0 support now; every new NSS acquisition must be CNSA 2.0-compliant starting January 1, 2027 — under three months out (about 11 weeks) — with exclusive use across operating systems, custom applications, and cloud services required by 2033. If you're bidding network or SATCOM gear into an NSS-designated environment, CNSA 2.0 support is table stakes, not a differentiator, and unlike the CMMC deadline above, nothing has paused this clock.
