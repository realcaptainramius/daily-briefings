Anthropic

Sep 15Google breaks precedent, opens Claude Opus 5 to all engineers

Google quietly changed internal policy to let every engineer access Anthropic's Claude Opus 5 through its internal Antigravity IDE, ending a longstanding rule that had mostly confined staff to Gemini. Google says "Gemini remains our primary and foundational model for internal development," but Claude is now available under quota for coding tasks — a tacit admission that Gemini still trails frontier coding models, and a notable vote of confidence in Claude from a direct competitor.

Techmeme / Business Insider · Dataconomy
Sep 15US agencies: China distilling Claude at industrial scale

The FBI, NSA, and CISA said top Chinese AI developers (DeepSeek, Alibaba, Moonshot AI, MiniMax, StepFun, Z.AI) have systematically mined Claude alongside ChatGPT, Gemini, and Grok since 2024 via "distillation," likely with Chinese government awareness. China's foreign ministry denied the accusation. Comes days ahead of Trump's Sept 24 meeting with Xi Jinping.

OpenAI

Sep 15OpenAI publicly confirms AI-safety coordination with Anthropic, Google; downplays antitrust risk

Global policy chief Chris Lehane said OpenAI's engagement with Anthropic and Google DeepMind on AI safety has been underway "for several weeks," moving the story from reported talks (covered yesterday) to an on-record confirmation. Lehane said OpenAI sees no need for an antitrust waiver for the three labs to coordinate, and that he was in Washington meeting lawmakers to back bipartisan catastrophic-risk legislation — a response to Dario Amodei's "pace the frontier" essay.
