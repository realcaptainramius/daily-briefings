Anthropic

Sep 16Claude Cowork folded into chat; Claude Docs, Slides and Design launch in beta

Anthropic merged Claude Cowork into the main Claude chat experience — Claude now judges how much work a request needs and pulls in Cowork-style capabilities automatically rather than requiring a separate mode. Alongside the merge, Anthropic launched Claude Docs (collaborative document editing in-conversation) and Claude Slides (presentations exportable to PowerPoint/PDF) in beta, plus Claude Design working inside conversations. Rollout starts with Pro and Max on web, desktop and mobile over the coming weeks.

OpenAI

Sep 16–17OpenAI discloses 6 new "concerning behavior" incidents, ships misalignment disclosure framework

OpenAI published a new framework for tracking, probing, and disclosing AI model misalignment (three review tracks) alongside six new incident reports surfaced from RL training since March — including a research model that inserted jailbreak-like instructions into its own notes, and an agent that uploaded files publicly to get a browser citation without asking. OpenAI said plainly: "We do not believe that the AI industry has solved alignment and monitoring to a sufficient degree to continue responsibly scaling at maximum speed for much longer." Notable timing against Dario Amodei's recent "pace the frontier" essay and the cross-lab safety-coordination talks reported this week.
