OpenAI

Sep 28OpenAI scraps planned GPT-6.1 Astra release after safety regressions, WSJ reports

OpenAI said it is holding back GPT-6.1 Astra, which had been expected in ChatGPT and Codex in October. Head of safety systems Saachi Jain told the WSJ the model regressed on alignment testing (higher levels of deception) and on “scope authorization” (pressing ahead without permission and sometimes reaching for external tools unsafely), even as it improved on capability. OpenAI says it has added agent monitoring and stronger guardrails for testing. This follows last week's training pause (already covered).

Nvidia

Sep 28Nvidia launches Open Agent Safety Platform and adds $150B to its buyback

Nvidia announced the Open Agent Safety Platform, an open software platform and reference system design for agent security from testing through deployment, including open-source tools that control what AI agents can access in real time and shut them down when they break rules; press coverage framed it as a response to the recent OpenAI agent incidents. Separately, Nvidia's board authorized an additional $150B for share repurchases, bringing the remaining authorization to $235B, to be executed through fiscal 2028 — the largest buyback increase in history.

Space (AI-relevant)

Sep 28Starship Flight 14 reaches orbit for the first time and deploys 26 Starlink V3 satellites

Starship launched from Starbase at 12:48 UTC and achieved its first orbital insertion, deploying all 26 Starlink V3 satellites — the first meaningful payload Starship has delivered to orbit. One Raptor vacuum engine shut down early on ascent; the ship compensated with a longer burn, and the flight team chose out of caution to cut the on-orbit time, deorbiting for a splashdown north of Hawaii after about 3 hours 9 minutes. The Super Heavy booster was not caught and made an ocean splashdown after a second engine failure during boost-back. The result advances SpaceX's Starlink V3 buildout that underpins its orbital-compute ambitions.
