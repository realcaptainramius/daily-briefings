Oct 18Anthropic commits 5% of its shares to a new Public Benefit Fund aimed at AI job disruption

Anthropic announced an Anthropic Public Benefit Fund, with 5% of company shares committed to it subject to stockholder approval, per CNBC and The Hill. The fund is aimed at workers displaced by AI. Oct 15Anthropic reportedly told investors Q3 revenue hit $16.8B and claimed adjusted profit; IPO still unconfirmed

Following its Oct 14 San Francisco investor day, CNBC, the Financial Times and Bloomberg (via secondary summaries) reported that Anthropic told attendees third-quarter revenue was $16.8 billion, up about 45% from roughly $11.6 billion in Q2, with an adjusted profit and adjusted gross margin near 85%. The profit is not GAAP net income and the adjustments were not disclosed. Valuation talk is inconsistent: earlier reports cited $1.8–2 trillion, while Reuters-based coverage says attendees discussed about $1.5 trillion. Marketing could reportedly start the week of Nov 9 with a debut before Thanksgiving. The figures come from people who attended the meeting; Anthropic has not published a prospectus or confirmed the timeline, and the original articles were not reviewed.
