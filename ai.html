Nov 16Anthropic shortens its vulnerability-disclosure deadlines to 30 and 45 days from 2027

Anthropic said it will cut its coordinated vulnerability disclosure windows for flaws its models find, effective for reports filed on or after Jan 1, 2027: high- and critical-severity flaws will be published 30 days after reporting and all others after 45 days, down from the 90-day norm. It will also escalate to an external coordinator after 15 days of maintainer silence (from 30) and wait 30 days after a patch (from 45) before releasing full technical details. Open reports keep their old deadlines, though serious flaws already past 90 days get 30 more days. Anthropic cites AI finding bugs faster than they can be fixed and says hard cases such as critical infrastructure will get negotiated timelines. Seen via search summaries of Anthropic’s post and coverage.
