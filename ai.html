Nov 23Anthropic launches Claude Ship to review, fix and approve GitHub pull requests

Anthropic introduced Claude Ship, which combines its generally available Code Review with two new beta agents. PR Steward pushes fixes for failing CI checks, merge conflicts and reviewer feedback, and is designed not to approve or merge. Claude Approvals applies an approval policy the team writes in the repository and can auto-approve routine changes or require named human sign-off; Anthropic advises starting in observe-only mode. It is in beta for Team and Enterprise plans on GitHub repositories and is not available to organizations using Zero Data Retention, the HIPAA configuration or customer-managed keys; developers on any paid plan can try a /ship command in Claude Code. Usage is billed on tokens, and eligible Enterprise organizations can claim at least $10,000 in credits by Jan 15, 2027. Anthropic warns it may miss bugs or security vulnerabilities.

Nov 20–21Anthropic mathematician posts a claimed Claude-assisted proof of the Riemann hypothesis as Lean code

Anthropic researcher Levent Alpöge released on Nov 20 a Lean 4 archive of about 175,000 files that he says is a machine-checked proof of the Riemann hypothesis and the Generalized Riemann hypothesis, developed “working with Claude.” Per coverage, the Lean FRO and Mathlib communities said on Nov 21 they have strong confidence the formal proof checks and that its theorem matches Mathlib’s standard statement, and MIT’s Andrew Sutherland and Oxford’s James Maynard are quoted as highly confident. Hours later, AMS president Ravi Vakil released a roughly 940-page human-written draft by Alessandro Masullo that also claims to prove the Generalized Riemann hypothesis; Masullo calls it unfit for submission, and John Baez noted on Nov 22 that its final chapter contains placeholders. No human-readable version of Alpöge’s proof has been published, Stanford’s Brian Conrad criticized releasing a proof its author does not fully understand, and one paper argues the formal statement is trivial. This is a claim that has not been peer reviewed; reports differ on how much credit Claude gets. Seen via search summaries only.
