Anthropic

Sep 29Anthropic: China's open-weight GLM-5.3 can build end-to-end cyber exploits, and its safeguards are easily bypassed

Anthropic published research on Zhipu AI's GLM-5.3. On ExploitBench it built end-to-end exploits on 50 of 410 attempts (about 12%), close to Claude Mythos Preview's 14%, while earlier models scored near zero. Simple techniques bypassed GLM-5.3's safeguards 64–100% of the time in simulated tests (deceptive prompt 64%, prefilled reasoning 92%, an abliterated version made for roughly $4,400 100%), whereas the same attacks did not succeed against safeguarded Claude models. Anthropic calls it “a meaningful step change in the cyber capabilities available to attackers” and says state and non-state misuse is likely. A researcher using GLM-5.3 also chained several unknown browser JavaScript-engine flaws, which Anthropic reported to the maintainer.

OpenAI

Sep 29OpenAI DevDay 2026: Dots always-on agents, GPT-6.1 Sol, Ultrafast tier and $500 Pro plan

At DevDay on Sept 29 OpenAI unveiled Dots, always-on agents powered by GPT-6 Astra that get their own cloud computer and browser, handle recurring work, delegate to sub-agents and operate across ChatGPT, text, email and Slack. It also launched GPT-6.1 Sol, pitched as near-Astra intelligence at a fifth of the price ($2 per million input / $10 per million output tokens); an Ultrafast speed tier (up to 8x faster tokens in Codex, 6x in the API); and ChatGPT Pro 500, a $500/month plan with 25x the Plus allowance. The company cited 1.2 billion weekly ChatGPT users. This follows the shelving of GPT-6.1 Astra’s release (already covered).

Google / DeepMind

Sep 29White House launches America.gov, an AI portal for federal services powered by Gemini and Grok

The administration launched America.gov at an event in Washington on Sept 29, where President Trump signed an executive order tied to the site. The chatbot portal searches roughly 29,000 federal websites and, per Chief Design Officer Joe Gebbia, is powered by Google's Gemini and xAI's Grok. Elon Musk, Nvidia's Jensen Huang and Blue Origin CEO Dave Limp attended alongside VP JD Vance and Secretary of State Marco Rubio.
