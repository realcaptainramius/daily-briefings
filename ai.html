Anthropic

Nov 10–19Anthropic prices record IPO at $83, above range, raising about $81.3 billion; offering due to close Nov 20

Anthropic said on Nov 18 that it priced its IPO of 980,000,000 Class A shares at $83.00 per share, above the $70–78 range it set at the Nov 10 roadshow launch and up from the 825 million shares first planned; 956,351,354 are sold by Anthropic and 23,648,646 by selling stockholders, and underwriters (led by Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley) have a 30-day option for up to 147,000,000 more. Reports say a concurrent $26 billion private placement of non-voting shares at the IPO price includes Nvidia (about $10 billion), Google and Microsoft, plus up to $500 million from TSMC per earlier reports. Before pricing, Bloomberg reported orders of about six to seven times the shares on offer. Per filing summaries, Amazon and Google would own about 15% and 14% after the offering, and quarterly revenue rose from about $11.6 billion in Q2 to about $17 billion in Q3. Trading began Nov 19 (next item). The offering was expected to close Nov 20 subject to customary conditions; no confirmation of closing or of the underwriters exercising their option had been seen at compile time. Pricing terms are from Anthropic’s release; valuation and placement details rest on search summaries of press coverage.

Nov 16Anthropic shortens its vulnerability-disclosure deadlines to 30 and 45 days from 2027

Anthropic said it will cut its coordinated vulnerability disclosure windows for flaws its models find, effective for reports filed on or after Jan 1, 2027: high- and critical-severity flaws will be published 30 days after reporting and all others after 45 days, down from the 90-day norm. It will also escalate to an external coordinator after 15 days of maintainer silence (from 30) and wait 30 days after a patch (from 45) before releasing full technical details. Open reports keep their old deadlines, though serious flaws already past 90 days get 30 more days. Anthropic cites AI finding bugs faster than they can be fixed and says hard cases such as critical infrastructure will get negotiated timelines. Seen via search summaries of Anthropic’s post and coverage.
