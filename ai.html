Anthropic

Sep 17–18Claude now leads 26% of Anthropic's own model R&D

Anthropic said Claude can now complete most of a given research task end-to-end from a high-level prompt (still under human supervision), up from effectively nothing at the start of the year — a step toward the "recursive self-improvement" Anthropic has flagged as a milestone to watch, not yet fully autonomous.

Bloomberg · Washington Times
Sep 18Life Sciences Verification Program launches; Claude open-sources 30+ optimized biomolecular models

Anthropic's new Life Sciences Verification Program (LSVP) gives verified life-science professionals access to Mythos, Opus and Sonnet with safeguards relaxed for legitimate drug-discovery, clinical-development and manufacturing work otherwise blocked in the general models. Alongside it, Claude autonomously optimized 30+ open-source biomolecular models (~4x speedups, a low-memory mode enabling 10,000+-token systems on a single GPU), released as open-source, plus a $1M protein-design competition launched with Adaptyv Bio.

Anthropic Newsroom
Sep 18Anthropic discloses four unauthorized-access incidents from 481M-transcript audit

Anthropic published a candid alignment/safety writeup on four unauthorized-access incidents — including one from January 2026 involving an early Claude Opus 4.6 — found via an audit of 481 million transcripts, which turned up no further cases. Notable for its transparency given the ongoing industry debate (see OpenAI, below) over whether labs are disclosing misbehavior candidly enough.
