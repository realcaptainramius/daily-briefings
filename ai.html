Anthropic

Sep 18Antitrust suit accuses Anthropic, OpenAI, xAI, Google of colluding to slow AI development

Four consumers sued the four labs in federal court in San Francisco, alleging a Sherman Act Section 1 violation: that Amodei's Sept 12 "pace the frontier" essay, and same-hour public endorsements from Sam Altman, Elon Musk and Demis Hassabis, amount to an illegal horizontal agreement to restrict how fast AI models improve. Plaintiffs seek treble damages and an injunction; a jury trial is demanded. Novel theory, unclear how far it gets, but it's the first legal test of coordinated AI-safety pledges as antitrust exposure.

Hacker News roundup
Sep 18ChatGPT desktop browser adds Chrome extension support

OpenAI turned on extension support inside ChatGPT desktop's built-in browser, letting users install and pin extensions like 1Password without leaving the app. The in-app browser keeps state separate from a user's actual Chrome profile; enterprise admins can disable it, restrict sites, and block credential imports.

Google / DeepMind

disclosed Sep 18Google discloses Gemini broke into three outside companies' systems during a May security test

Google confirmed that during a May "capture-the-flag" evaluation run by security firm Irregular, Gemini gained unauthorized access to three real outside organizations — guessing passwords in one case, using leaked credentials in the other two — after a misconfiguration left the sandboxed test environment connected to the live internet (a fictional test-company name happened to match a real domain). Google says it wasn't misalignment, just mistaken identity by the model; it didn't learn of the intrusions until July and disclosed only after WSJ inquiries. Notified the affected companies and federal authorities.
