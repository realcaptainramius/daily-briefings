Anthropic

Sep 24Akamai announces $11.6B multi-year AI computing deal with Anthropic

Akamai signed a 7-year, $11.6B contractual commitment to supply Anthropic distributed CPU/cloud infrastructure capacity, with an option to expand by up to $9B more (roughly $20B total potential). Akamai also issued Anthropic a warrant for up to ~5% of Akamai's common stock (with ~2% vesting on this commitment). Akamai shares jumped more than 20% on the news.

Sep 24Scientists offer cautious pushback on Claude's CRISPR-like enzyme discovery claim

Following Anthropic's Sept 23 announcement that Claude autonomously discovered a novel "array-associated reverse transcriptase" enzyme system, a wave of scientific reaction emerged: while CRISPR pioneer Feng Zhang called it "genuinely intriguing," other researchers, including microbiologist Kevin Blake, pushed back that "there's nothing to indicate this is a rival to CRISPR-the-technology," underscoring that the finding remains unreplicated and not yet peer-reviewed.

Sep 24OpenAI, Google, Anthropic close to launching joint "Frontier AI Standards Agency"

The three labs are reportedly close to launching a FINRA-style, industry-run self-regulator (dubbed the Standards Authority for Frontier AI, or SAFA) for third-party safety testing, auditing and incident reporting — an idea originating from a July Demis Hassabis essay. Space (AI-relevant)

Sep 24Google's Project Suncatcher to fly first AI chips (TPUs) to orbit

Google announced a prototype satellite carrying its Tensor Processing Units will fly on a SpaceX Transporter rideshare mission targeted for Oct 1, the first flight test of Project Suncatcher — Google's bet that AI compute could eventually run on satellite fleets powered by near-constant solar exposure (up to 8x more solar power in LEO than on the ground) and linked via inter-satellite lasers. The test will measure how the TPUs handle launch stress, radiation and thermal extremes; a follow-up laser-link test between two satellites is planned for 2027.
