Anthropic

Sep 23Anthropic opens first public biology lab; says Claude autonomously discovered a novel CRISPR-like enzyme system

Anthropic announced a California-based public biology lab (BSL-1/BSL-2) and said Claude autonomously discovered a new enzyme system in bacteriophage DNA — "array-associated reverse transcriptases" (ART) with CRISPR-like repeats — using roughly 950 agents over 21 hours across 210M tokens, screening more than 200,000 candidate reverse transcriptases down to 20. CRISPR pioneer Feng Zhang (MIT) called the finding "genuinely intriguing"; the work is not yet peer-reviewed.

Sep 23Sam Altman addresses UN Security Council, calls for international AI-risk standards

Altman told the UN Security Council that no level of catastrophic AI risk is acceptable and called for international standards for measuring frontier AI capabilities, assessing risk, and preserving human oversight, arguing such decisions should ultimately rest with democratically accountable governments. Anthropic's Dario Amodei joined via video for the same session.
