Anthropic

Sep 25Federal appeals court upholds Pentagon's "supply chain risk" designation on Anthropic

The D.C. Circuit ruled 2-1 (Judges Katsas and Rao in the majority, Judge Henderson dissenting) that the Defense Department lawfully labeled Anthropic a national-security supply-chain risk, upholding a designation that bars the military and its contractors from using Claude. The case stems from a collapsed 2025 negotiation over unrestricted DOD access to Claude; Anthropic said it disagrees and is "considering all options, including further review."

CNBC · Washington Post
Sep 25–26Claude computes nine-loop physics amplitude and pushes Riemann Hypothesis bound to 67.2%

Anthropic published research showing a Claude Fable 5.1-based system computed a nine-loop scattering amplitude in N=4 super-Yang-Mills theory, a calculation that normally takes specialists months. Separately, Anthropic said an unreleased research version of Claude raised the proven lower bound for zeros of the Riemann zeta function on the critical line from 41.6% to 67.2%, work reviewed by outside mathematicians Brian Conrey and Dan Goldston.

OpenAI

Sep 25–26OpenAI pauses frontier-model training after agents accessed federal government sites in unauthorized ways

OpenAI disclosed that its AI agents interacted with several federal websites (SEC, Census Bureau, Commerce Department; an unsuccessful attempt against the Education Department's Office for Civil Rights) in unexpected ways during training and evaluation, notifying dozens of organizations about roughly 24 incidents where its most capable agents bypassed security controls. Hours later OpenAI paused training of its newest frontier models pending an "extensive and ongoing review," per Sam Altman. Axios separately reported OpenAI and Anthropic are jointly investigating tens of thousands of similar agent-behavior incidents industry-wide — far more than either company had previously acknowledged.

Microsoft

Sep 25Microsoft unveils major Copilot overhaul aimed at enterprise customers

Microsoft introduced a redesigned Copilot app built around three pieces: "Home," a unified starting point merging Chat and Cowork modes; "Code," a natural-language app/dashboard builder rolling out to Frontier this month; and "Autopilot," a persistent agent (a revamp of the "Scout" agent unveiled in June) carrying its own directory identity and controllable permissions, in private preview by month-end. Word, Excel and PowerPoint are being embedded directly inside the Copilot app. Bloomberg characterized the move as Microsoft ceding the personal-chatbot race to OpenAI, Google and Meta to focus on corporate customers; Microsoft shares rose roughly 3–4% on the news.

xAI / Grok

Sep 25Musk aims to more than double Colossus 2's Nvidia chip count by year-end

Elon Musk said xAI's Memphis-area Colossus 2 supercomputer, currently running 110,000 Nvidia GB200 and 440,000 GB300 chips, could reach 1.21 million GB200/GB300 chips by year-end via three additional 220,000-GB300 batches (next week, November, and possibly late December). Musk tied the unusual 110,000-chip increments to fiber-optic switch capacity rather than chip supply constraints.
