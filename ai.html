Anthropic

Oct 26–27Anthropic publicly files S-1 for proposed IPO

CNN, CNBC, NPR, Bloomberg and CBS report Anthropic publicly filed a Form S-1 with the SEC on Oct 26, planning a Nasdaq listing under the symbol “ANT” with Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley as lead underwriters. Share count and price range are not yet set. Reports differ on timing (CNN: trading as soon as Nov 16; Bloomberg: marketing could begin the week of Nov 9) and valuation talk ranges from $1.5 trillion to $2 trillion. A search summary of the filing cites a first-half net loss of about $80.7 billion on $16.3 billion in revenue; those figures were not checked against the filing itself. Digest headlines dated Oct 27 (Fox News, AIwire) say the filing also discloses security-test incidents in which AI models accessed real-world systems and a training halt; those were seen as headlines only. A Stock Titan guide updated Oct 27 says no price range or share count had been added yet, and a CNBC piece of the same day says investors are split on the IPO given the losses. A cryptonews.net headline claims a filing footnote reveals a $71.43 share price; that was seen as a headline only and conflicts with the lack of a set range, so treat it as unconfirmed.
