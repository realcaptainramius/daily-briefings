Cisco

RecentAI Network Assistant reaches general availability in Catalyst Center

Cisco's AI Network Assistant, embedded in Catalyst Center, is now generally available — letting network engineers query network state, diagnose issues, generate configurations, and get change recommendations using natural language. Paired with Duo IAM's new support for AI agents (identity-aware access control and accountability mapping between agents and human owners), this is Cisco pushing agentic AI into both the network and identity layers of its stack, both areas WWT integrates heavily.
