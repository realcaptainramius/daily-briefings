Microsoft

Sep 23Growth margins discovery and eligibility APIs go live, ahead of Oct 1 partner-economics overhaul

Microsoft's new "growth margins" model — a structural, ongoing part of CSP partner economics that programmatically rewards partners for qualifying customer growth (new-to-offer, seat expansion, or strategic mix) — became discoverable and validatable via API and the Partner Center Pricing workspace on Sep 23, with actual growth-margin transactions beginning Oct 1. Directly relevant to WWT's own Microsoft partner economics; worth confirming eligibility ahead of the cutover. Frontier Accelerate for Marketplace (noted previously) remains the latest consolidated partner-program news.
