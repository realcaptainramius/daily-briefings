Cisco

Oct 7WebexOne 2026: agents in Webex chats, Claude Managed Agents for Webex, RoomOS 27

At WebexOne in Austin, Cisco announced collaborative AI agents that can be @mentioned in Webex group spaces (scheduling, notes, task tracking), Anthropic's Claude Managed Agents for Webex, and RoomOS 27 for meeting-room hardware (due in November, adding native Google Meet support alongside Webex, Teams and Zoom). Dialog, an agentic customer-experience harness, enters beta in early 2027. Most features are roadmap items planned for early 2027 and pricing for the new AI add-on was not disclosed — relevant mainly to WWT collaboration and contact-center refresh conversations. The Supermicro rack-scale compute addition to the Secure AI Factory with NVIDIA (announced Aug 25) begins shipping this month.

Computerworld · UC Today
Oct 12–15Cisco and NVIDIA propose USFP optical interface at OCP

Cisco and NVIDIA put forward a proposed USFP optical interface for AI networking, with Google collaborating on Falcon Transport. The design targets roughly 4x the bandwidth density of traditional 200Gb/s-per-lane pluggable optics and about 1.3x better power efficiency. This is a proposal, not a shipping product.

Dell Technologies

Oct 1Dell Pro Rugged 13 Extreme and Dell Pro Rugged 14 laptops announced

New fully rugged laptops (6-foot drop rating, IP66, 1,400-nit displays, Intel Core Ultra) replace the older rugged branding — mainly relevant to field-workforce and public-sector device deals. Separately, Dell, JERA and RHAELM signed an MoU on a standardized framework for national-scale AI infrastructure in Japan. Record fiscal Q2 FY27 results (noted previously) remain the latest financial results. On Oct 14 Barclays downgraded Dell (along with HPE and NetApp) to Equal Weight over enterprise AI demand doubts, and Dell shares fell about 11% — market sentiment rather than a product or partner-program change.

Hewlett Packard Enterprise

Sep 30Networking Investor Day: FY27 networking growth outlook raised; first AMD Helios order ($1.2B from Vultr)

HPE lifted its fiscal 2027 networking revenue growth outlook to the high-teens to low-20s percent (from 14–17%) and disclosed a $1.2B order from Vultr for AMD Helios AI Rack systems — its first for the platform — including HPE Juniper Networking scale-up switching. Juniper integration synergies are targeted at $800M annual run-rate by FY28. Relevant to WWT AI-networking and rack-scale conversations. Oct 14: Barclays downgraded HPE to Equal Weight and shares fell with Dell; SDxCentral also reports HPE is weeks away from folding its Juniper-based networking business into its overhauled partner program (undated, single source).

NetApp

Sep 29–Oct 1INSIGHT 2026: Novus AI factory architecture, Keystone Sovereign, OCI-native ONTAP; WWT named NA Partner of the Year and AI Partner of the Year

NetApp unveiled Novus, a storage architecture for AI factories and neoclouds that runs ONTAP on dense data nodes with metadata split into a separately scalable pNFS layer. Also announced: Keystone Sovereign (data and support kept in Europe, starting in Germany and France in November), a planned fully managed ONTAP service in Oracle Cloud Infrastructure, AI Data Engine metadata discovery on non-NetApp storage, and GA of the Nutanix integration. On Oct 1 NetApp named its FY26 North America Partner Award winners, with WWT taking both North America Partner of the Year and AI Partner of the Year. Oct 14: Barclays also downgraded NetApp to Equal Weight and shares fell. Separately, NetApp announced its intent to acquire PEAK:AIO (reported Sep 25; terms undisclosed, subject to regulatory approvals) to fold its metadata, global namespace and parallel NFS capabilities into ONTAP for large GPU clusters.

NVIDIA

Oct 12–15OCP Global Summit: Vera Rubin rack and compute tray designs contributed to OCP (MGX 1.2), 800 VDC and Spectrum-6 Ethernet

At the OCP Global Summit in San Jose, NVIDIA contributed its Vera Rubin rack and compute tray designs to the Open Compute Project through the MGX 1.2 architecture, which adds blind-mate liquid cooling, standardized power and cooling interfaces, native 19-inch equipment support and a liquid-cooled 5,000-amp busbar. Coverage also describes continued push on 800 VDC power architecture, with Meta adopting Spectrum-6 Ethernet. Relevant to WWT AI factory design, power and cooling conversations; shipment timing for Vera Rubin systems was not covered in the sources reviewed. NVIDIA reports fiscal Q3 earnings in mid-November.

NVIDIA Blog · Converge Digest · Tech Edition
