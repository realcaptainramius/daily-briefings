Cisco

Nov 9AI Defense adds frontier red teaming with Anthropic's Claude Mythos; Splunk Agent Observability and Cisco IQ data in Splunk

Missed in last week's edition. Cisco is adding Anthropic's Claude Mythos to Cisco AI Defense for adaptive red teaming of customers' own AI applications and agents — initially for select enterprises, with broader access on a when-and-if-available basis; standard red teaming is already available. Each engagement is authenticated, tied to a named operator and logged for audit. On the Splunk side, enhanced Tokenomics ties agent cost to output quality using Amazon Bedrock and CloudWatch data (private preview in December, AWS Marketplace in early 2027), and Cisco IQ vulnerability and configuration data now flows into the Splunk Platform. Relevant to WWT AI security and Splunk observability conversations. No new Cisco press announcements were found for Nov 16–22.

Cisco Newsroom · Network World
Nov 12Fiscal Q1 2027: record $18.5B revenue (+24%); FY27 outlook raised, hyperscaler AI target lifted to $9B

For the quarter ended Oct 24, Cisco reported revenue of $18.5B (product +32%, services +1%) and non-GAAP EPS of $1.40, above its $18.0–18.2B guidance. Product orders rose 21% and networking orders 28%, with AI-related Nexus data center switch orders up 47%. Cisco raised full-year FY27 guidance to $74.8–75.8B revenue and $5.47–5.53 non-GAAP EPS (from $72.2–73.4B and $5.05–5.11), lifted its FY27 hyperscaler AI infrastructure revenue expectation to about $9B (from $7.5B), and guided Q2 to $18.0–18.2B. Hyperscaler AI orders reportedly dipped sequentially, which Cisco described as lumpy, and the stock fell about 6% the next day. For WWT, strong enterprise networking orders support campus and data center refresh demand.

Dell Technologies

Nov 16SC26: Dell AI Factory adds Agentic Data Gateway, liquid-cooled Exascale storage and rack-scale management software

At SC26 in Chicago, Dell expanded the Dell AI Factory (with NVIDIA, and with AMD GPU options per Blocks & Files). The new Agentic Data Gateway gives AI agents a single governed path to enterprise data; Dell's internal testing claims up to 76% fewer tokens and accuracy rising from 60% to 94% when agents reuse data context (vendor figures). Dell also added a liquid-cooled 2OU Exascale storage platform (up to 32 NVMe SSDs, running ObjectScale, PowerScale or Lightning software) and management software for up to 100,000 devices. OpenManage Rack Scale, Fabric Manager and the PowerEdge XE9785 with NVIDIA HGX B300 were listed as available now; other components ship later, with a validated modular configuration slated for Q4 2026. Relevant to WWT AI factory storage and rack-scale operations conversations. Fiscal Q3 results are expected in late November, but no date has been confirmed by Dell in search results.

NVIDIA

Nov 17Fiscal Q3 2027: revenue $115.8B (+103%), data center $107.6B; Vera Rubin ramp near $20B; Q4 guide $130B

In its first report under interim CEO Colette Kress, NVIDIA posted revenue of $115.8B for the quarter ended Oct 25 (up 20% sequentially), with data center revenue of $107.6B (+110% year over year) driven by the initial Vera Rubin ramp, which coverage puts at nearly $20B in its first quarter of production shipments, with racks running at CoreWeave, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure, OCI and Nebius. For WWT, the numbers point to Vera Rubin becoming the default platform in AI factory designs through 2027; leadership remains interim.
