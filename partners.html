Hewlett Packard Enterprise

Sep 30Networking Investor Day: FY27 networking growth outlook raised; first AMD Helios order ($1.2B from Vultr)

HPE lifted its fiscal 2027 networking revenue growth outlook to the high-teens to low-20s percent (from 14–17%) and disclosed a $1.2B order from Vultr for AMD Helios AI Rack systems — its first for the platform — including HPE Juniper Networking scale-up switching. Juniper integration synergies are targeted at $800M annual run-rate by FY28. Relevant to WWT AI-networking and rack-scale conversations.

NetApp

Sep 29–Oct 1INSIGHT 2026: Novus AI factory architecture, Keystone Sovereign, OCI-native ONTAP; WWT named NA Partner of the Year and AI Partner of the Year

NetApp unveiled Novus, a storage architecture for AI factories and neoclouds that runs ONTAP on dense data nodes with metadata split into a separately scalable pNFS layer. Also announced: Keystone Sovereign (data and support kept in Europe, starting in Germany and France in November), a planned fully managed ONTAP service in Oracle Cloud Infrastructure, AI Data Engine metadata discovery on non-NetApp storage, and GA of the Nutanix integration. On Oct 1 NetApp named its FY26 North America Partner Award winners, with WWT taking both North America Partner of the Year and AI Partner of the Year.
