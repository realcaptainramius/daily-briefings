Cisco

Nov 12Fiscal Q1 2027: record $18.5B revenue (+24%); FY27 outlook raised, hyperscaler AI target lifted to $9B

For the quarter ended Oct 24, Cisco reported revenue of $18.5B (product +32%, services +1%) and non-GAAP EPS of $1.40, above its $18.0–18.2B guidance. Product orders rose 21% and networking orders 28%, with AI-related Nexus data center switch orders up 47%. Cisco raised full-year FY27 guidance to $74.8–75.8B revenue and $5.47–5.53 non-GAAP EPS (from $72.2–73.4B and $5.05–5.11), lifted its FY27 hyperscaler AI infrastructure revenue expectation to about $9B (from $7.5B), and guided Q2 to $18.0–18.2B. Hyperscaler AI orders reportedly dipped sequentially, which Cisco described as lumpy, and the stock fell about 6% the next day. For WWT, strong enterprise networking orders support campus and data center refresh demand.

At Cisco's 30th Partner Summit, WWT was named Global One Cisco Partner of the Year, plus Global Enterprise, Global Americas and Global Networking Partner of the Year (per WWT's release). Oct 12–15Cisco and NVIDIA propose USFP optical interface at OCP

Cisco and NVIDIA put forward a proposed USFP optical interface for AI networking, with Google collaborating on Falcon Transport. The design targets roughly 4x the bandwidth density of traditional 200Gb/s-per-lane pluggable optics and about 1.3x better power efficiency. This is a proposal, not a shipping product.

NetApp

Nov 10Nebul becomes first neocloud to adopt NetApp Novus for sovereign AI; Q2 FY27 results set for Dec 1

NetApp said European AI-native cloud provider Nebul is the first neocloud customer for Novus, the AI factory storage architecture launched at INSIGHT 2026 that separates metadata from the data path. NetApp Newsroom · ITWeb
Nov 2NetApp completes PEAK:AIO acquisition to fold metadata and parallel file capabilities into Novus

NetApp announced it has closed its acquisition of PEAK:AIO (announced in late September; terms undisclosed), planning to integrate PEAK:AIO's metadata services and parallel namespace technology into its ONTAP-based Novus architecture for large AI and GPU-as-a-service deployments. NetApp says the deal gives existing ONTAP customers an evolution path; product timing remains at NetApp's discretion. Relevant to WWT AI factory storage design conversations.

NetApp Newsroom · HPCwire · SDxCentral
