No significant updates this window. New Glenn remains grounded following the May 28 static-fire explosion at Cape Canaveral's Launch Complex 36 (root cause: BE-4 engine failure); Blue Origin still targets a return to flight before year's end, now planning a horizontal/vertical hybrid launch operations concept using existing infrastructure rather than rebuilding the damaged pad.

SpaceX

Sep 26–27FAA grants final launch license for Starship Flight 14

The FAA issued the launch/reentry license for Starship Flight 14 late Saturday, resolving the last open regulatory item and keeping the mission on track for a 75-minute window opening 8:15 a.m. EDT Monday, Sept 28 from Starbase's Pad 2. The flight plan calls for the vehicle's first-ever orbital insertion (~275 km, ~6 orbits), deployment of 26 Starlink V3 satellites, a deorbit burn on a single Raptor engine, and a controlled reentry and splashdown in the Pacific; backup dates of Sept 29–30 are available if needed. As of this briefing's compilation the launch outcome was not yet known.
