Firefly Aerospace

Sep 22Firefly completes new cleanroom to accelerate spacecraft production

Firefly held a ribbon-cutting for a new cleanroom at its 144,000-sq-ft Cedar Park, TX campus, four times the size of its existing one. Combined, the two ISO Class 8-rated cleanrooms create an assembly line able to support up to 12 lunar landers/orbital vehicles simultaneously, backed partly by a Texas Space Commission grant; Firefly cites five additional Blue Ghost lander/Elytra orbiter missions under contract.

Starcloud

Sep 22–23Starcloud to attempt first Bitcoin mining in space aboard Starcloud-2

CEO Philip Johnston announced Starcloud will fly Bitcoin-mining ASICs aboard Starcloud-2, betting that near-continuous orbital solar power undercuts mining's main terrestrial cost. Most coverage still describes the launch as targeted for "later this year," consistent with the previously reported October window, though some reporting suggests a possible slip into early 2027 — not yet confirmed by Starcloud directly.
