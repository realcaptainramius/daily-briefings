Blue Origin

Oct 16Blue Origin plans new Florida launch pads, targets multiple New Glenn launches a week by end of decade; return to flight still pending

A Reuters report, syndicated by a local radio site, says Blue Origin aims for several New Glenn launches per week by decade’s end, with a $330 million facility expansion targeted for summer 2027. The rocket has reportedly been grounded since a May explosion at its launch pad, with return to flight expected early next year; NASASpaceflight separately describes a year-end target for the rebuilt LC-36 pad, and the dates may slip. These are company goals, not commitments. Spaceflight Now (Oct 16) reports the plan includes new Florida launch pads, and CEO Dave Limp reportedly still sees a path to a launch this year; no return-to-flight date is confirmed. Oct 15SpaceX asks FAA to allow up to 456 Starship launches a year from South Texas

Space.com reports SpaceX has asked the FAA to raise the Texas Starship limit from 25 to as many as 456 launches a year; the FAA review is at an early stage. Flight 14 flew Sept 28. On Oct 10 Musk said Flight 15, which would attempt the first ship catch, was “four to six weeks” away and would probably fly a little before Thanksgiving; one outlet puts an FAA planning date at Nov 8. This supersedes earlier Oct 19 tracker dates, and SpaceX has announced no official date. The FAA request and Musk’s timing are not decisions; no approval or launch date is confirmed. Details come from a search summary of the article.
