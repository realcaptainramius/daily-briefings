Oct 1SpaceX targets Oct 1 Falcon 9 launch of NASA’s Crew-13 to the ISS

SpaceX and NASA cleared Crew-13 after the final readiness review, targeting liftoff at 11:10 a.m. EDT Oct 1 from Cape Canaveral SLC-40 (backup Oct 2), with forecasts of a 55–60% chance of acceptable weather. The crew — NASA’s Jessica Watkins and Luke Delaney, CSA’s Josh Kutryk and Roscosmos’ Sergey Teteryatnikov — is to dock at Harmony’s forward port about nine hours later for a mission of roughly six to seven months.
