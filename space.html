Blue Origin

Oct 16Blue Origin plans new Florida launch pads and a much higher New Glenn flight rate; return to flight reported slipping to early 2027

Spaceflight Now (Oct 16) reports Blue Origin plans additional Florida launch pads, and Reuters (via a local radio site) says it targets several New Glenn launches a week by decade’s end. A secondary outlet reports an FAA request to raise cadence from 12 to 50 launches a year. New Glenn has been grounded since a May 28 static-fire explosion damaged its only launch site. Bezos had aimed for December 2026, but Reuters-based coverage now points to early 2027; Blue Origin has not announced a return-to-flight date, and NASASpaceflight separately describes a year-end target for the rebuilt LC-36. These are company goals, not commitments. Details come from search summaries.

Oct 15SpaceX asks FAA to allow up to 456 Starship launches a year from South Texas

Space.com reports SpaceX has asked the FAA to raise the Texas Starship limit from 25 to as many as 456 launches a year; the FAA review is at an early stage. Flight 14 flew Sept 28. On Oct 10 Musk said Flight 15, which would attempt the first ship catch, was “four to six weeks” away and would probably fly a little before Thanksgiving; one outlet puts an FAA planning date at Nov 8. This supersedes earlier Oct 19 tracker dates, and SpaceX has announced no official date. The FAA request and Musk’s timing are not decisions; no approval or launch date is confirmed. Details come from a search summary of the article.
