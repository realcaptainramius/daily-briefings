SpaceX

Sep 23SpaceX stacks Starship and Super Heavy ahead of Flight 14, wet dress rehearsal set for Sept 24

SpaceX stacked Ship 41 atop Super Heavy Booster 21 on the Starbase orbital launch mount early Sept 23, assembling the full 124-meter vehicle ahead of Flight 14 — the vehicle's first full orbital-flight attempt. A wet dress rehearsal (cryogenic propellant load without ignition) is planned no earlier than Sept 24 to validate ground systems and hardware ahead of the Sept 28 launch target, which would deploy an operational stack of upgraded Gen-3 Starlink satellites; the FAA has not yet granted a launch license.

Relativity Space

Sep 23FAA issues environmental clearance for Terran R launches at SLC-16

The FAA's Office of Commercial Space Transportation signed a Finding of No Significant Impact and Record of Decision (dated Sept 17) for Relativity Space's Terran R launch operations at Space Launch Complex 16, Cape Canaveral Space Force Station. The FAA adopted the Space Force's 2024 Final Supplemental Environmental Assessment rather than conducting a new study, authorizing up to 24 launches per year on azimuths of 41–105 degrees. The finding clears an environmental/regulatory step toward Relativity's still-pending Part 450 vehicle operator license; it does not itself grant the launch license.
