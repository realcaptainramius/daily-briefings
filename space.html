Oct 7–8Crew-12 splashes down off California after 8 months in space

NASA’s Dragon Freedom undocked from the ISS Harmony module at 8:05 a.m. ET Oct 7 and splashed down in the Pacific off California on Oct 8 (reported at about 11:34 a.m. ET), carrying Jessica Meir, Jack Hathaway, ESA’s Sophie Adenot and Roscosmos’ Andrey Fedyaev. The recovery ship Shannon hoisted the capsule with the crew inside before they were flown ashore. Reported time aboard the ISS differs by outlet (235–237 days).

Oct 5–10SpaceX’s SDA T1TL-A launch (21 Northrop satellites) re-set for early Oct 10 after Oct 5 scrub

The Falcon 9 launch from Vandenberg SLC-4E auto-aborted with about 58 seconds on the clock Oct 5; SpaceX said vehicle and payload were healthy but gave no cause. Spaceflight Now (Oct 9) reported a retry set for 12:39 a.m. PDT Saturday Oct 10 with booster B1103 planned to land at LZ-4. Secondary outlets (tech-insider.org, Neoteo, Basenor) report liftoff at 12:39 a.m. PDT Oct 10 with B1103 landing at LZ-4; no post-launch SDA or SpaceX statement was found, so treat it as reported but not confirmed by a primary source.
