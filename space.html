SpaceX

Sep 15Starship Flight 14 confirmed for Sept 22 — first orbital-velocity attempt, first revenue-generating flight

SpaceX confirmed Flight 14 for Sept 22 (75-minute window opening 7:15am CT), with the Starship upper stage attempting to reach orbital velocity for the first time. The flight will deploy the first batch of 26 third-generation Starlink satellites — making it the first Starship mission to generate revenue for SpaceX, even if that revenue flows from Starlink rather than the launch business itself. SpaceX says it's made further hardware and software changes to the booster to address issues from the last flight.

Sep 15Axiom signs MoU with ESA on post-ISS infrastructure

Axiom Space and the European Space Agency signed a memorandum of understanding to explore cooperation on space infrastructure, research, and commercial LEO activities after the ISS retires, including a possible European Cargo Return service and potentially a European Crew Vehicle to help transport crew and cargo to Axiom Station.
