SpaceX

Sep 14Starship Flight 14 slips again to NET Sept 22; catch attempt deferred to Flight 15

Flight 14 — the first Starship V3 flight and SpaceX's first attempt to reach orbital velocity with the new configuration — is now targeting no earlier than Sept 22 at 12:15 UTC from Starbase Pad B, a further slip from the Sept 18 window reported last week. The stack pairs Super Heavy Booster 21 (full-duration static fire completed Aug 28) with Ship 41 (six-engine static fire completed Aug 21); both vehicles are expected to attempt soft ocean splashdowns rather than a tower catch, with Musk pegging the catch attempt on Flight 15 at 50–60% odds of success.
