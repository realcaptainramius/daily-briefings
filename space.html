Blue Origin

Nov 5–7Blue Origin reported to push New Glenn return to flight to January 2027

Multiple outlets (The Next Web, NewsBytes, Gagadget, New Space Economy dated Nov 7) report that a memo from CEO Dave Limp tells staff the next New Glenn launch slips from December 2026 to January 2027, blaming the rebuild of Launch Complex 36 at Cape Canaveral and bad weather. The pad was damaged in a May 28, 2026 static-fire explosion and is the company’s only New Glenn pad. No specific launch date was given, and the next flight is reported to carry Amazon Leo satellites; a Blue Moon MK1 lander flight is listed for Q1 2027 and could slip. An earlier Oct 29 SatNews report of a November target is superseded. Seen via search summaries only; no Blue Origin statement was reviewed.
